Camera Access Required

SmartFace needs access to your camera to recognize faces and record attendance. Camera access was denied or is unavailable on this device.

  1. Click the camera / lock icon in your browser's address bar.
  2. Select Allow for camera permissions.
  3. Refresh this page (F5 or Ctrl + R).
  4. If the icon is not visible, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and allow access for this site.

Note: Camera access requires the site to be served over localhost or HTTPS.
